What am I looking at?

Each dot is one tracked object at its current position, worked out in your browser from its published orbital elements with the standard SGP4 model. The day and night sides follow the Sun's actual position; the twilight band between them is a simplified blend. Earth imagery: NASA Blue Marble and Black Marble.
